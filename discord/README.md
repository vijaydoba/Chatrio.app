## Rules and safety message

The following message is published and pinned in `#rules-and-safety`.

> 🛡️ **Chatrio Community Rules & Safety**
>
> Welcome! To keep this community safe and useful:
>
> 1. **Adults only (18+).** Chatrio and this community are only for people
>    aged 18 or older.
> 2. **Be respectful.** No harassment, hate speech, threats, bullying, or
>    targeted abuse.
> 3. **Protect privacy.** Never post private information, passwords, precise
>    locations, intimate images, or another person’s personal data.
> 4. **No sexual or exploitative content.** No explicit content, sexual
>    solicitation, non-consensual material, or content involving minors.
> 5. **No spam or scams.** No phishing, malware, impersonation, repeated
>    promotions, or misleading links.
> 6. **No illegal or dangerous activity.** Do not encourage harm, violence,
>    fraud, or unlawful behavior.
> 7. **Use the right channels.** Keep conversation relevant; use **#support**
>    for help, **#bug-reports** for reproducible issues, and **#feedback** for
>    ideas.
> 8. **Follow Discord’s rules and Chatrio’s Terms:**
>    https://chatrio.app/terms
>
> **Stay safe:** Block and report suspicious or abusive users. Do not move
> conversations off-platform or share personal details until you trust the
> other person. Moderators may remove content or members to protect the
> community.

## Public roadmap

The roadmap is published in `#roadmap`.

> 🗺️ **Chatrio Roadmap**
>
> Here’s what we’re building toward. This roadmap is directional, and
> priorities may change based on safety, quality, and community feedback.
>
> ✅ **Live now**
>
> - Instant anonymous one-on-one chat — no sign-up required
> - Real-time text and image messages
> - Topic-based matching when shared interests are available
> - Skip/Next, typing indicators, delivery receipts, and live online counts
>
> 🔨 **Improving next**
>
> - Stronger safety and moderation tools
> - Better mobile performance, reliability, and chat polish
> - Continued improvements to Circles — nearby anonymous conversations and
>   local groups
> - Community support and faster feedback through Discord
>
> 🧪 **In development**
>
> - **Blind Date (18+):** compatibility-based matching with identities hidden
>   until mutual reveal
> - A native Circles Android experience with push notifications
> - Better notification support across devices
>
> 🔭 **Longer-term ideas**
>
> - Voice messages
> - Video chat after mutual reveal
> - AI-powered conversation starters
> - Travel mode and advanced matching preferences
>
> Have an idea or feature request? Share it in **#feedback**. 💬
