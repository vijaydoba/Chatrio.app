# Chatrio — Directory Submission Plan (July 2026)

Goal: first 25–30 legitimate referring domains in 30 days. Baseline (2026-07-12): ~0 referring
domains, 25 ranked keywords, ~2 organic visits/month. Every listing below is free unless marked.

**Tracker:** `submission-tracker.csv` in this folder. Log every submission the day you make it.

---

## Rules (read once)

1. **Never paste the same description twice.** Use the copy variants below and rotate the
   opening sentence. Directories and AI engines down-weight duplicate text.
2. **Link target:** homepage `https://chatrio.app` for general directories; the Omegle
   alternatives post for anything Omegle-specific.
3. **After a listing goes live**, verify the link and log it in the tracker.
4. **No paid link packages, no submission services, no DR<10 spam directories.** Free +
   editorial only. (One flag on this domain already — keep the profile clean.)

## Copy Variants

**Tagline (under 10 words):**
- A: `Anonymous chat with strangers — no sign-up, nothing stored.`
- B: `Free random text chat. No registration, no video, no logs.`

**Short description (~60 chars):**
- A: `Free anonymous 1-on-1 text chat with strangers. No sign-up.` (59)
- B: `Talk to strangers anonymously. Free, instant, no account.` (58)

**Long description — variant 1 (startup/launch directories, lead with outcome):**
> Chatrio pairs you with a random stranger for a one-on-one text conversation in seconds —
> no account, no email, no phone number. Pick a few interests, click New Chat, and you're
> talking to a real person. Conversations aren't stored: when you close the chat, it's gone.
> There's no video, which keeps the experience safer and less exposed than camera-first
> random chat sites, and interest matching means you're more likely to land in a conversation
> you actually want to have. Chatrio also includes Circles — anonymous local chat rooms based
> on proximity, so you can talk to people nearby without sharing who you are. It's free,
> works in any browser on any device, and there's nothing to install. Built for people who
> miss what random internet conversation used to feel like, minus the worst parts.

**Long description — variant 2 (alternative/SaaS directories, lead with the Omegle angle):**
> When Omegle shut down in November 2023, millions of people lost the simplest way to talk
> to a stranger online. Chatrio is a text-first replacement: instant one-on-one anonymous
> chat with no registration, no phone number, and no chat logs. Unlike video-first
> alternatives (OmeTV, Chatroulette), Chatrio is text-only — no camera exposure, no explicit
> content risk — with interest-based matching so conversations start with common ground.
> Reporting tools are one click, moderation is active, and nothing you say is stored after
> the chat ends. It runs in any browser, free, on desktop or mobile. If you used Omegle for
> the conversations rather than the cameras, this is the closest thing to that experience
> in 2026.
