Online, nobody knows your reputation.

There's no social context, no history, no image to maintain. You can just be whoever you actually are right now.

The parts of you that don't fit elsewhere fit here.

The niche interests, the unusual opinions, the things you edit out in person — they're welcome in a conversation with someone who just met you.

Strangers judge less than people who know you.

People who know you filter what you say through who they think you are. Strangers just hear what you actually said.

Use the freedom.

Say the thing you'd normally keep to yourself. Try the side of you that doesn't get much air. See how it lands.

 Be fully yourself
