Endings matter as much as openings.

How you end a conversation is the last impression you leave. A good ending can make even a mediocre chat feel meaningful. A bad one can undo an hour of great conversation.

👻

Don't just disappear.

Ghosting mid-conversation — even with a stranger — leaves a bad feeling. A simple "I need to go but this was genuinely good" takes five seconds and leaves both people feeling better.

💬

Acknowledge something specific.

"The thing you said about X actually made me think" is a much better goodbye than "nice talking to you." Specific acknowledgment shows the conversation mattered.

🚪

Leave the door open if it was good.

"I hope we end up talking again" or "remember this if you're ever on here again" — these phrases honour a good conversation without making promises you can't keep.

🎁

A good ending is a gift.

Most online conversations just stop. Being the person who closes gracefully is memorable. It's a small thing that says: this mattered to me. Start your next chat on Chatrio.
