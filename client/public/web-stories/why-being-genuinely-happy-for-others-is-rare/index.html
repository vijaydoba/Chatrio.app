Most people hold back when others succeed.

When someone is genuinely delighted by your good news — no jealousy, no deflection — you feel it immediately. It creates deep trust.

Celebrate loudly.

'That's incredible, tell me everything' after someone shares good news is one of the most bonding things you can say.

Generosity of spirit is a choice.
