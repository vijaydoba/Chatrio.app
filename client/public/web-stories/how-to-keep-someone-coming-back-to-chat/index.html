The best conversations have a pull.

Some chats end and the other person is already thinking about the next one. Here's what creates that pull.

🎣

Leave something unfinished.

'I'll tell you the rest of that story another time' is a small investment in a future conversation. People return for what they're curious about.

🌟

Make them feel great about themselves.

People return to sources of positive feeling. If talking to you leaves them feeling interesting and understood, they will come back.

🎬

End at the high point.

Don't let a great conversation fizzle. End it when the energy is still good. Leave them wanting more, not less.

🧲

Be consistent, not intense.

Showing up warmly and reliably over multiple conversations is more powerful than one incredible chat followed by silence.
