Everyone says the wrong thing sometimes.

A clumsy joke, a misread tone, a poorly timed comment. It happens to everyone. What matters is what you do next.

Acknowledge it simply.

'Sorry, that came out wrong' fixes more than over-explaining ever could. A clean, brief acknowledgment resets the moment.

Don't spiral.

Over-apologizing makes things more awkward, not less. Address it once, then move forward naturally. The other person already has.

Recovery shows character.

Anyone can be smooth when things go well. Handling a misstep with grace is far more impressive — and more human.

 Just be human
