You don't need perfect words.

We freeze waiting for the perfect thing to say. But conversation isn't a performance — it's a back-and-forth that forgives imperfection.

Start before you're ready.

The right words often come once you start typing, not before. Action unlocks them. Waiting for certainty keeps them hidden.

Imperfect and honest beats polished and fake.

A clumsy real message connects more than a flawless calculated one. People respond to authenticity, not eloquence.

Trust yourself to find them.

In the flow of a real conversation, the words show up. Relax, stay present, and let them come.
