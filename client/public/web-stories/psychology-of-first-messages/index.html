The first message sets everything.

Before a conversation has even started, the first message has already told the other person whether you're worth their time. Here's what's actually happening in their brain.

⚡

People decide in under 3 seconds.

Research on first impressions shows decisions form before conscious processing kicks in. In text, that 3-second window is your opening line. It needs to create curiosity or warmth — not both, just one.

🗑️

Generic triggers pattern recognition.

"Hey" or "How are you" activates the same part of the brain that handles spam. It's been seen a thousand times and the brain categorises it as low-value instantly.

💡

Specific triggers genuine response.

A specific, unexpected opener forces the brain to actually process it. It can't be auto-responded to. That processing creates engagement — and engagement creates conversation.
