Some chats start frozen.

Short replies, dry energy, going nowhere. Before you skip, try thawing it out — many cold chats just need a spark.

🔥

Raise your own energy first.

Energy is contagious. If you bring warmth and curiosity, the other person often rises to meet it.

🚪

Ditch the yes/no questions.

"Did you have a good day?" gets 'yeah.' "What made today good or bad?" gets a real reply. Open the door wider.

💡

Find what lights them up.

Mention a few topics until one sparks energy. Everyone has something they love to talk about — find it.

➡️

You tried. Not every chat is meant to catch fire. Skip with zero guilt and find a warmer one.
