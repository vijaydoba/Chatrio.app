Quiet doesn't mean closed.

A quiet person often has the richest inner world. They're just waiting to feel safe enough to share it.

Share first.

Going first with something a little personal shows them the water's warm. Vulnerability invites vulnerability.

Ask specific, easy questions.

Broad questions overwhelm. 'What's been the best part of your week?' is easier to answer than 'tell me about yourself.'

Reward every opening.

When they share something small, respond with warmth. They're testing whether it's safe to share more. Show them it is.
