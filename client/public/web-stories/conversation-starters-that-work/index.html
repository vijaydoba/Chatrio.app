"Hey" doesn't start conversations. It starts waiting.

After "hey" someone has to do the actual work of beginning the conversation. A real opener already contains the beginning of something. Here are ones that actually work.

🎲

Ask something they haven't been asked before.

"What's something you're good at that would surprise people?" or "What's the last thing you got genuinely excited about?" Unusual questions get unusual answers — and that's where real conversations begin.

🎣

Give them something to react to.

"I just had the most bizarre conversation with someone and I need a second opinion" opens a conversation because now they're curious. Give them a hook before you ask for anything.

🌐

Reference your shared context.

On a random chat platform you're both strangers who chose to be here. "What made you open this app tonight?" is a genuine question with no wrong answer that gets to something real fast.

👂

Then actually listen.

The best opener in the world means nothing if you don't respond to what they say. The opener gets them talking. Listening keeps them talking. Try it on Chatrio now.
