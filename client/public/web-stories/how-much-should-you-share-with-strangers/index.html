Anonymity has a sweet spot.

Share too little and the conversation stays shallow forever. Share too much too fast and you create real risk. The skill is finding the middle.

💭

Feelings are safe to share early.

How you feel about something, what you think, what you're into — these build connection with almost zero risk attached.

🔒

Identifying details should wait — maybe forever.

Full name, exact location, workplace, phone number — these aren't necessary for a good conversation and carry real risk with a stranger.

🪜

Trust builds in layers, not all at once.

Good conversations naturally deepen over time. There's no rush to share everything in the first ten minutes.
