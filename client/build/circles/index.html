How Circles keeps you safe
  • Approximate location only. Your position is snapped to a coarse area — never your exact spot, and never shown on a map.
  • You stay anonymous. No email or phone number — just a nickname you choose.
  • How we know it's you without an account: your browser gets a random anonymous ID stored only on your device. Your nickname and avatar are attached to that ID — clear your browser data and the identity is gone for good.
  • You control who reaches you. People can send one intro message; you accept or decline. Declining blocks them.
  • Report & block anytime. Reporting also blocks the person and alerts our moderators.
  • Adults only. Circles is strictly 18+.

A local chat app that stays anonymous

Circles is a free local chat app for meeting people near you without giving up your privacy. There's no sign-up — pick a nickname, share an approximate location, and see who's around. You send one intro message; if the other person accepts, the chat opens. If they decline, they never hear from you again.

Nearby chat, groups, and intro requests

Talk to people near you one-on-one, or start a local group pinned to your area — a coffee meetup, late-night talks, a running crew. Everything is anonymous, moderated, and strictly 18+.

How can it remember me without a sign-up?

When you first use Circles, your browser generates a random anonymous ID that never leaves your device except to identify your session. Your nickname, anime avatar, and chats are linked to that ID — not to an email, phone number, or real name. Come back on the same device and browser and Circles recognizes you; clear your browser data and that identity is erased permanently.
