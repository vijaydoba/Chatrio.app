The gap between chat and real life is real.

Online connection is valid — but at some point, if it's becoming important, it needs to move. Here's how to do that without wrecking what you have.

📞

Start with voice, not video.

A voice call adds tone and pace that text can't carry. It's more revealing than text but less pressure than video. It's the right first step.

🎥

Video comes next.

Video adds body language, eye contact, how someone laughs. If feelings survive a video call, they're real. If they don't — that's useful information too.

☕

Meet in public. Stay smart.

First in-person meetings should be public, during the day, with someone who knows where you are. This isn't paranoia — it's just sense.

✅

The connection either deepens or clarifies.

Meeting in person doesn't always make things better. But it always makes things clearer. Either way, you've got something real to work with.
