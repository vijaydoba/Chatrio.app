Worth talking to isn't about being impressive.

It's not your job history, your wit, your looks. It's whether someone feels better after talking to you.

People worth talking to make you feel interesting.

Not because they compliment you — because they're genuinely curious. Their attention makes you come alive.

They carry something real.

Opinions, observations, curiosities — they bring a perspective. Not performed, not borrowed — genuinely their own.

You already are, if you show up honestly.

You don't have to become someone worth talking to. You have to stop hiding the version of you that already is.

 Show up honestly
