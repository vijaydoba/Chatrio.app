Good conversations die from small mistakes.

It's rarely one big thing that ends a chat. It's a pattern of small, avoidable errors that drain energy from both sides until someone stops replying.

🪞

Mistake 1: Making it all about you.

Every answer that pivots to your own story without acknowledging theirs first is a small withdrawal from the relationship. Do it enough and the account empties.

🎤

Mistake 2: Firing questions without sharing.

An interview is not a conversation. If you're asking all the questions and sharing nothing about yourself, the other person feels interrogated. Balance it.

😐

Mistake 3: One-word replies.

"lol", "yeah", "nice" — these responses tell someone their message wasn't worth a real reply. Even if you're busy, a short genuine response beats a polite dismissal.

💪

The fix: show up fully.

Read the whole message. Acknowledge it before responding. Share something real in return. Do this consistently and conversations don't die — they grow. Try it now on Chatrio.
