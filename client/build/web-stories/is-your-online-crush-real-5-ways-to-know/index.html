You like them. But is it real?

Online connections can feel intense fast. Before you catch feelings you can't take back, here's how to know if what you have is genuine.

1. They're consistent over time.

Real people show up the same way every time you talk. Their stories match. Their personality doesn't change. Inconsistency is always a red flag.

2. They're curious about your real life.

Genuine people ask real questions. Fake connections stay surface-level. If they never ask who you actually are, that's telling.

3. They don't avoid reality.

Real people talk about their day, their mood, their life. They're not always perfect. If someone never has a bad day online, that's a performance.

4. They make you feel safe, not chased.

Genuine connections don't pressure you to move fast. If someone rushes intimacy or asks for things too quickly — slow down and pay attention.
