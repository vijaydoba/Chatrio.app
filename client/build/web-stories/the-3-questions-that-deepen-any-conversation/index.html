Most conversations need just a little push.

Three questions can take any conversation from pleasant surface chat to something genuinely memorable.

Question 1: What do you actually think about that?

Not what's popular or expected — what they genuinely believe. It invites a real person to show up instead of a curated one.

Question 2: What was that like for you?

The feeling behind the fact. Moving from what happened to how it was experienced is the bridge from surface to depth.

Question 3: What do you really want?

Beneath every situation is a desire. Asking this — gently, at the right moment — opens something that polite conversation never reaches.
