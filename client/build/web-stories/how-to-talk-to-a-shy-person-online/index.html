Shy doesn't mean uninterested.

Some of the best conversations are with quiet people who just need a little room to open up. Here's how to give it.

🪴

Ask easy questions first.

Don't open with something heavy. Light, low-pressure questions let a shy person warm up at their own pace.

🛟

Share first to lower the wall.

When you go first with something a little personal, you signal it's safe. Shy people open up to people who feel safe.

⏳

Be patient with the pauses.

Don't fill every silence or fire off three messages in a row. Give them space to think and reply.

💎

Quiet people often go deepest.

Once a shy person trusts you, they often share the most thoughtful things. The patience pays off.
