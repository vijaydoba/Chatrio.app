Silence in chat feels worse than it is.

Running out of things to say isn't a failure — it's what happens when you're treating conversation like an interview instead of an exploration. Here's how to fix it.

🌊

Go deeper, not wider.

When conversation stalls, most people jump to a new topic. That's the wrong move. Go deeper into what was just said. Ask the question behind the question.

🗝️

Use the FORD method.

Family, Occupation, Recreation, Dreams. These four areas never run dry. And every answer opens three more doors if you actually listen to what comes back.

💡

Share something random.

"Random thought — have you ever noticed..." or "This is unrelated but I've been thinking about..." Random observations are conversation gold. They show a real brain at work.

🔭

The real secret: stay curious.

People who never run out of things to say aren't smarter or funnier. They're just more genuinely interested in the person in front of them. Curiosity is infinite. Use it on Chatrio.
