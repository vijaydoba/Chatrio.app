Both feel the same in the moment.

When you really need to talk, it can be hard to know where helpful venting ends and exhausting oversharing begins.

Venting has a purpose.

You're releasing pressure, gaining perspective, feeling heard. When venting works, you feel lighter. The other person feels useful.

Oversharing goes past the point.

It's when you've been heard — but kept going. More detail, more repetition, more emotional weight than the relationship can hold.

Ask before you unload.

'Is it okay if I vent for a bit?' is one of the kindest things you can do for someone. It gives them a choice before you pour in.

Check in after.

After you've vented, ask: 'How are you doing?' It signals that you see them as a person, not just a container for your feelings.
