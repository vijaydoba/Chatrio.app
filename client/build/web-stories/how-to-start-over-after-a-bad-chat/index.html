One bad chat isn't a verdict.

A conversation that went nowhere — or sideways — says nothing about your worth or your ability. It was just one chat among thousands possible.

Don't carry it into the next one.

The biggest mistake is letting a bad chat make you guarded for the next person. They didn't cause it. Start fresh.

Reset your energy first.

Take a breath. Close the app for a minute. Come back when you're not carrying frustration from the last conversation.

The next stranger is a clean slate.

They know nothing about what just happened. To them, you're a fresh start. Give yourself the same gift.
