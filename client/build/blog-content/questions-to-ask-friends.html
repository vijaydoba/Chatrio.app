We spend a lot of energy on romantic conversation and almost none on the friendships that quietly hold our lives together. That's a mistake. Good friends are made — and kept — by the same thing that makes any relationship real: a mix of laughter, honesty, and genuine curiosity about each other.

Here are 90 questions to ask your friends, from the gloriously stupid to the surprisingly deep. Use them on a road trip, over a long dinner, in a group chat that's gone quiet, or with a new friend you're still getting to know.

Why Questions Make Friendships Stronger

Communication researcher Jeffrey Hall found in a 2018 study that friendship runs on time: it takes roughly 50 hours together to become casual friends, about 90 to become real friends, and more than 200 hours to become close. But not all hours are equal. Hall found that meaningful conversation — not just being in the same room — is what actually deepens a bond. In other words, a great question can turn an ordinary hangout into one that counts double.

📊 The Friendship Shortcut

  • Hours together build friendship — but quality of conversation accelerates it.
  • Fun and depth both matter: laughter builds comfort, honesty builds trust.
  • The best friend questions invite stories, not one-word answers.
  • Asking is a gift — it tells someone you're genuinely interested in them.

Fun & Hilarious Questions

Start here. Nothing bonds people faster than laughing together.

  1. What's the most embarrassing thing you've done sober?
  2. What's a hill you'll absolutely die on?
  3. What's your most-used emoji, and what does it say about you?
  4. What weird thing do you do when no one's watching?
  5. What's the worst haircut you've ever had?
  6. If your life were a sitcom, what would the title be?
  7. What's a completely useless fact you'll never forget?
  8. What's the strangest thing you've eaten and actually liked?
  9. What's your villain origin story?
  10. What app do you waste the most time on?
  11. What's a trend you fully fell for and now regret?
  12. What's your most irrational fear?
  13. What would you do if you were invisible for a day?
  14. What's the pettiest reason you've stopped talking to someone?
  15. What's a lie you told as a kid that you got away with?
  16. What's the dumbest way you've been injured?
  17. What's your go-to karaoke song, no shame?
  18. What's the weirdest compliment you've ever received?
  19. What's a food combination you love that others find gross?
  20. What celebrity would you swap lives with for a week?

  4. Who was your first best friend, and are you still in touch?

  6. What's a family tradition you loved?
  7. What's the most trouble you got into as a kid?
  8. What game did you play obsessively growing up?
  9. What's a memory from school you'll never forget?
  10. What's something you were obsessed with that you're embarrassed about now?
  11. Who was your first celebrity crush?
  12. What's a place from your childhood you'd love to revisit?
  13. What was your bedroom like growing up?
  14. What's a lesson from your childhood that still guides you?
  15. If you could relive one day from your teenage years, which would it be?

Hypotheticals & Debates

Great for a group — these start the kind of arguments friends love having.

  1. Would you rather be able to fly or be invisible?
  2. If you could have dinner with any three people, who?
  3. Is a hot dog a sandwich? Defend your answer.
  4. Would you rather always be 10 minutes late or 20 minutes early?
  5. If you had to delete one app forever, which one?
  6. Would you rather know how you die or when you die?
  7. What would you do with a year off and unlimited money?
  8. If you could master one skill instantly, what would it be?
  9. Would you rather live without music or without movies?
  10. What's the best decade of music, and why are you right?
  11. If you could live in any fictional universe, which one?
  12. Would you rather be famous or be the best in the world at something no one knows?
  13. What superpower is secretly useless?
  14. If animals could talk, which would be the rudest?
  15. Would you rather redo the last five years or skip the next five?

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best questions for a new friend?

Start with the Fun and Nostalgia sets — they build comfort without pressure. Save the Deep set for once you've logged some real hours together and the trust is there. Rushing to heavy questions with a brand-new friend can feel intense.

How do I ask deep questions without making it awkward?

Answer first. If you want to ask a friend something vulnerable, share your own answer to the same question before you ask theirs. It signals that this is a two-way exchange, not a spotlight — and it makes opening up feel safe.

Which questions work best for a group?

The Hypotheticals and "How Well Do You Know Me?" sets are built for groups — they spark debate and friendly competition. Deep questions are usually better one-on-one, where people feel free to be honest.

Can these help me make friends online?

Yes. Meaningful questions are exactly how online acquaintances become real friends. On Chatrio you can practice with a stranger in seconds — no sign-up — and see how fast a fun question turns into a genuine conversation.

The Bottom Line

Friendships don't deepen by accident — they deepen through hours together and the honest, funny, curious conversations that fill them. Keep a few of these questions handy, and turn your next ordinary hangout into one that actually counts.
